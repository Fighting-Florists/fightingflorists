Understanding Awareness through Color.

Our Ribbons Explained.

Posted: November 10, 2025

Every flower holds a purpose, and every color reflects a story of strength.

In this blog, we share the meanings behind our roses and the powerful causes they support. We hope these symbols inspire kindness, connection, and understanding for those facing difficult journeys.

Cancer = Lavender

Our lavender roses shine in support of cancer awareness, representing courage, positivity, and a light to guide those on their journeys. Our goal for our lavender roses is to spread positivity to each person facing cancer, to acknowledge them, and to support them. Fighting Florists are eager to take part in the cancer support system; we do this by shining out a light of support and admiration for all people facing cancer. We believe that when they glance at our flower, they are reminded of the support and love their supporters have for them.

If you’re interested in joining cancer support systems/programs, visit:

Cancer Support Chalmers
Cancer.ca
Partnership Against Cancer

If you need help or support, visit:

Cancer.gov
Cancer.ca

Mental Health = Forest Green

Our green roses glow to promote mental health awareness, creating a safe space for each person facing mental health issues. Fighting Florists believe that mental health should be recognized more than it already is. We resolve this problem by spreading awareness and supporting each person who goes through any mental health diagnosis. We believe that when each person glances at our flower, they'll have a sense of peace and support.

If you need help, don't hesitate to call or text 9-8-8 or visit:

CMHA General Info
CMHA National Programs
CMHA - How to Get Help
CMHA - Helping Others

Autism = Baby Blue

Our blue roses beam to support autism awareness, aiming to promote understanding and acceptance of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). Our goal is to encourage and celebrate all autistic individuals and their unique strengths. Fighting Florists believe each autistic individual is filled with beauty, power, and love.

Learn more here:

Autism Canada
AIDE Canada

Suicide Prevention = Yellow

Our yellow roses spread awareness for suicide prevention. Fighting Florists believe suicide needs to be talked about more in our communities. This flower spreads awareness to everyone, regardless of gender, ethnicity, or background. Suicide is a leading cause of death worldwide, but small acts of awareness can help someone who is struggling.

If you need help, call or text 9-8-8 or visit:

CMHA National Programs
CMHA Mental Health Info

Heart and Stroke = Red

Our red roses symbolize our support for Heart and Stroke Awareness. We aim to honor those fighting against heart and stroke issues. Research shows around 50% of Canadians are unaware of Heart and Stroke Awareness Month, so we want to elevate awareness and support.

Learn more or get support:

Heart & Stroke Foundation
Heart Disease Resources
CPR Training

Breast Cancer = Pink

Our pink roses bloom proudly for breast cancer awareness. They stand for hope, strength, courage, and love. We want to support all women facing breast cancer and celebrate their resilience. Our goal is to share positivity and uplift them on their journey.

Resources and support:

BreastCancer.ca
Cancer.ca — Breast Cancer Info
Canadian Breast Cancer Network
